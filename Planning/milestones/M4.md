# M4 — Playable moves and move reports

Status: **frozen at checkpoint 1** on branch `m4` (2026-10-23). The Captain's decisions
(§1, P1–P10; P2, P7 and P9 revised the same day) are fixed; she approved the
Deputy's refinements (R1–R7; R8 withdrawn) and the changed concept diagrams on

changes: the orchestrator makes them, records them in §15 and tells the Captain.

Intent: before any language model, the output answers the question the user asked, move
by move, and leaves out what does not bear on that move. The output shows **playable
moves**, never "candidate moves"; verdicts use a two-bucket scale; "tactic" means a
line that mates or wins material, and the old margin rule becomes **opportunity**.
Acceptance intent: every rule below has a fixture or a test that fails on today's
behaviour, and every fact in a move report is a field of the full report.

Milestones renumbered (Captain, 2026-10-23): M4 = playable moves and move reports
(this plan), M5 = language-model prose, M6 = books, M7 = native apps.

## 4. Rules

Notation: `S` the side to move, `O` the opponent; loss in es_units (1/2000), as today.

### 4.1 Search A and the selection

- `--moves` grammar: a comma-separated list without spaces; each item is a keyword
  (`best`, `playable`, `inaccuracies`, `mistakes`, `blunders`, `unplayable`, `all`),
  `topN` (N a decimal integer ≥ 1), or a move in SAN or UCI that is legal in the
  analysed position. Keywords are lower case and cannot be read as SAN. Input errors
  (exit 2): an empty item (`--moves Nf3,,e4`, a trailing comma) →
  `empty item in --moves`; `top0` or `top` → `invalid value for --moves: <item>`; an
  item that is neither a keyword nor a legal move → today's error for an illegal or
  unreadable move. A move named twice (e.g. `Nf3,g1f3`) is reported once.
- No `--moves` means `--moves best`. `--moves` given twice is the existing
  `duplicate flag` error.
- **Whole-board keywords**: `playable`, `inaccuracies`, `mistakes`, `blunders`,
  `unplayable`, `all`. Each needs a verdict for every legal move.
- K = the number of legal moves when the selection holds a whole-board keyword;
  otherwise max(5, N) for the largest `topN` (capped at the legal moves), 5 when there
  is none.
- A's nodes = `node_budget(budget).a` × K / 5 (integer arithmetic, exact for today's
  budgets). `engine.multipv` reports K; `engine.nodes.A` the scaled value.
- Asked moves (the moves in the `--moves` list) outside A's K lines each get search C,
  as `--move` did; with a whole-board keyword no move is outside A.
- **Selected moves** (the union, each once): `best` → A's line 1; `topN` → A's first
  N lines, whatever their verdict; `playable` → verdict best or okay; `inaccuracies`,
  `mistakes`, `blunders` → that verdict; `unplayable` → inaccuracy, mistake or blunder;
  `all` → every line of A; a move → that move (from A or search C). Order: A's
  MultiPV order, then moves from search C in the order typed. A terminal position
  selects nothing; a keyword that matches no move selects nothing (not an error).
- **Detailed lines**: A's first min(K, 5) lines and every selected move. D, E and R
  run for detailed lines only (R4).
- `--help` lists the keywords and states, once, that the whole-board keywords search
  every legal move and take several times the default time, so they are for when every
  such move is needed (Captain, 2026-10-23).
- `playable_truncated` = A returned all K lines, K < legal moves, and every line is
  playable (renamed from `fine_truncated`, same logic on the new scale). Always false
  with a whole-board keyword.
- Output flags (P9): format and content are independent. No flag → move report
  text (§10.1); `--json` → move report document; `--full-report` → full report text
  (§10.2); `--json --full-report` → full report JSON. `--full-report` honours `--moves`: the
  selection sets K, the asked moves and the detailed lines, and `input.selection`
  records it.

### 4.2 Verdict

1. loss ≤ 100 units (0.05) → playable:
   - **best** iff the move is one of A's lines and its engine score equals line 1's:
     both centipawn scores and equal `cp`, or both mate scores and equal `mate`;
   - **okay** otherwise.
2. 100 < loss ≤ 200 (0.10) → **inaccuracy**.
3. loss > 200 → **blunder** iff the move allows a tactic (§4.3), else **mistake**.

Line 1 is always best. A refutation is set iff the verdict is not playable.

### 4.3 Tactic

- Every move assessment gets `consequence`: the existing consequence rule applied to
  the move's own line from the analysed position, for `S` (signed material; `mate` only
  for a mate by `S`). Source rule.
- The move's line **is a tactic** iff `consequence.cls` is `mate` or `material`.
- The move **allows a tactic** iff `consequence.material ≤ −1` or the move's `eval.mate`
  is negative (R1).
- A refutation **is a tactic** iff the refuted move allows a tactic. B's and E's threat
  lines get no tactic flag (their consequence class already says it).
- Limits, documented: the walked plies (6, up to 10 while capturing) bound what is
  seen; best replies are the engine's at the search budget; R5.

### 4.4 Opportunity

The PostM2_EM1 §4.1 judgement, renamed: judged lines (A's line 1 and every refutation
of a detailed line), best alternative, only-move margin, forced. `is_opportunity` iff
not forced and margin ≥ `kOpportunityMarginUnits` (260, 0.13 ES). Lines of non-detailed
moves get no judgement.

### 4.5 Tactic lines, contributions and labels

A **tactic line** is a line that is an opportunity or a tactic. On every tactic line
(move lines and refutations), today's contribution rule (PostM3_EM2 §4.2) judges the
line's motifs and `tactic_labels` holds its `removal_of_defender` labels. For a move
line that is a tactic line, `root_motifs` copies `position.motifs` with contributions
judged against that move's line (today this is done for A's line 1 only, on
`position.motifs` itself, which becomes uncontributed). Contributions on other lines
stay null.

### 4.6 Relevance filter (move report)

A move report is built from one `MoveAssessment` of the full report and the report's
shared fields, by a pure function. It holds:

| Field | Kept when | From |
|---|---|---|
| rank, san, uci, eval, depth, loss, verdict, gives_check | always | the assessment; rank = A line number, null for a move from C |
| line, walked_plies, consequence, is_tactic, allows_tactic | always | the assessment |
| opportunity | the assessment has one | the assessment |
| motifs | contribution direct or indirect | the assessment's `motifs` |
| root_motifs | contribution direct or indirect | the assessment's `root_motifs` |
| tactic_labels | always (empty unless a tactic line) | the assessment |
| positional | net change: changes with `holds_at_end`, the last one per feature identity | the assessment's `positional` |
| parried_threat | `parries_threat.parries` is true | `O`'s threat from `threats` (line and consequence; no motifs) and the parry test |
| created_threat | class mate, material or promotion | `creates_threat` (line and consequence; no motifs) |
| refutation | verdict not playable | line, consequence, is_tactic, opportunity, tactic_labels; motifs with contribution direct or indirect |
| games | the source found the position and lists the move | its count in `games.by_source[0].moves` |

The document header holds `input`, `engine`, the position summary (fen, side to move,
in check, legal moves, terminal, `eval_white`), `selection` (the selection, lines
searched K, playable count, `playable_truncated`), `opening` and the game source
(id, table, platform, players, months, credit, found, games; no move list). Dropped:
other moves, the side to move's own threat, urgency, `position.motifs` and
`position.positional` (root facts reach a move only through `root_motifs`), threat-line
motifs, contributions of `none`.

### 4.7 Not in scope

Ordering moves by anything but A; filtering by level; hiding facts by importance beyond
§4.6; book or game prose.

## 5. Report v6

Full report (`document: "full"`):

- `schema_version` 6; `document` "full" (first key after `schema_version`).
- `input.moves`: `--moves` exactly as typed, or null (replaces `input.move`).
- `input.selection`: `{"keywords": [...], "top": int|null, "moves": [uci, ...]}`;
  keywords in the fixed order `best`, `playable`, `inaccuracies`, `mistakes`,
  `blunders`, `unplayable`, `all`; `top` the largest N; `moves` the asked moves in
  typed order, each once. No flag records `{"keywords": ["best"], "top": null,
  "moves": []}`.
- `asked_moves`: the asked moves outside A, one assessment each (search C), in typed
  order (replaces `asked_move`).
- `verdict` enum `best`, `okay`, `inaccuracy`, `mistake`, `blunder`.
- `MoveAssessment`: `consequence` (rule), `is_tactic` (rule), `allows_tactic` (rule),
  `detailed` (rule), `opportunity` (replaces `tactic`; same object without `labels`,
  `is_tactic` → `is_opportunity`), `tactic_labels`, `root_motifs`.
- `Threat`: `is_tactic` (nullable; set iff the threat is a refutation), `opportunity`
  (replaces `tactic`), `tactic_labels`.
- `fine_truncated` → `playable_truncated`.
- `position.motifs` carry no contribution (moved to `root_motifs`, §4.5).
- When `detailed` is false: `parries_threat`, `creates_threat`, `creates_threat_skipped`
  and the refutation's `opportunity` are null.

Move report document (`document: "moves"`): §4.6, one schema `moves.v6.schema.json`;
every field has a description and its provenance.
